Escape Velocity

At radius r: v_esc = √(2GM / r).

At the Schwarzschild radius this equals c. Inside, nothing — not even light — escapes.

📐 Maths Guide

Parametric Curves

Orbit: r(t) = a(cos nt, sin nt), n = 2π / T. Speed is |r′(t)| = na.

Line Integral = Risk

R = ∫ h(r(s)) ds where h is the hazard field. The agent compares R across different routes.

Gradient

∇h points toward increasing danger. The safest route stays orthogonal to steep peaks.

Calculus of Variations

Minimise ∫(1 + λh) ds over all paths. Solved numerically with Dijkstra on a 140×140 grid.

Orbital Transfer

Vis-viva: v² = μ(2/r − 1/a); Hohmann transfer time: T = π√(a³/μ) from Kepler’s law.

Tsiolkovsky Equation

Δv = v_e ln(m₀ / m_f). Integrating engine thrust gives the propellant mass ratio.

Derivatives in Relativity

Time dilation: √(1 − r_s / r), Lorentz factor: 1 / √(1 − v²/c²).

Synodic Period

1/S = |1/T₁ − 1/T₂|. Sets the cadence between optimal Hohmann transfer launch windows.
