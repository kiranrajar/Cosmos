Parametric Curves in 3D Space

Helical & orbital trajectory: r(t) = ⟨x(t), y(t), z(t)⟩ = ⟨a·cos(nt), a·sin(nt), c·t⟩ where n = 2π / T.

Velocity vector: v(t) = r′(t) = ⟨-na·sin(nt), na·cos(nt), c⟩. Speed: |r′(t)| = √(n²a² + c²).

In COSMOS: Propagates 3D planetary orbits and interplanetary spacecraft trajectories across heliocentric coordinates.

Quadric Surfaces · Ellipsoids

Equation: x²/a² + y²/b² + z²/c² = 1.

Parametric form: x = a·cos(u)cos(v), y = b·cos(u)sin(v), z = c·sin(u) where u ∈ [-π/2, π/2], v ∈ [0, 2π).

In COSMOS: Models planetary oblate spheroids (Jupiter/Saturn centrifugal flattening) and gravitational celestial body geometries.

Quadric Surfaces · Paraboloids

Elliptic: z/c = x²/a² + y²/b². Hyperbolic: z/c = x²/a² - y²/b².

Parametric form: x = a·u·cos(v), y = b·u·sin(v), z = c·u².

In COSMOS: Defines parabolic escape velocity trajectories (vis-viva parabolic limit) and high-gain spacecraft antenna geometries.

Quadric Surfaces · Hyperboloids

One sheet: x²/a² + y²/b² - z²/c² = 1. Two sheets: -x²/a² - y²/b² + z²/c² = 1.

Parametric form (one sheet): x = a·cosh(u)cos(v), y = b·cosh(u)sin(v), z = c·sinh(u).

In COSMOS: Governs hyperbolic gravity-assist slingshots, interstellar flybys (Voyager), and relativistic black hole ergosphere geometries.

Quadric Surfaces · Elliptic Cones

Equation: z²/c² = x²/a² + y²/b².

Parametric form: x = a·u·cos(v), y = b·u·sin(v), z = c·u.

In COSMOS: Renders Mach cones, relativistic light cones (spacetime causal boundaries), and sensor telemetry field-of-view.
